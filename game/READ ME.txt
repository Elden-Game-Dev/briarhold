THE BEACON OF BRIARHOLD

Double-click "Play Briarhold.html" to play in Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
That file contains the entire game. No installation, server, account, or
internet connection is needed. The browser must support WebGL 2.

WASD / Arrow keys: move relative to the screen
Space: swing your sword (nearby enemies help guide your aim)
Shift: dodge; briefly avoids damage
E: open a nearby chest or relight the final beacon
P / Escape: pause or resume

Recover the three Dawn Sigils shown as gold dots on the map. Defeat each
sigil's nearby guardians to collect it. The castle gate opens after all three.
Defeat the Hollow Warden in the courtyard, then press E at the crystal in
front of the great hall. Dodge when the Warden's attack ring appears.

Hearts restore two health. Green bottles give a temporary speed boost.
Chests contain coins and healing. Sigils also restore one health.

Source files are included for learning and editing:
index.html - page structure
style.css - interface styling
renderer.js - original native WebGL renderer, meshes, lighting, shadows
game.js - original world art, controls, combat, objectives, and game rules

All graphics and sounds were created in code for this game. No third-party
libraries or external game assets are used.
