## Full description

Beyond the old river stands a sleeping castle. Its beacon has gone dark.
Your journey begins with a sword, a quiet valley and three missing Dawn Sigils.

**The Beacon of Briarhold** is a small, single-player 3D adventure you can play
right in your browser. Wander through a village, cross the river's bridges,
search forest ruins and discover a hidden garden. Collect coins, open chests,
and face the creatures guarding the valley's light.

Recover all three sigils to open the castle gate. Then challenge the Hollow
Warden and relight the beacon.

- A colourful original world viewed from a fixed, angled camera.
- Sword combat, dodging and a castle boss with warning signals to watch for.
- Healing hearts, temporary speed boosts and hidden treasures.
- Original graphics and sound created in code.
- Free browser play, with no account or installation needed to play.

**Controls:** WASD or arrows to move · Space to swing your sword · Shift to dodge
· E to interact · P or Escape to pause. Toggle sound with the on-screen button.

**Requirements:** Desktop or laptop, keyboard and a WebGL 2 browser. No touch or
gamepad controls in this version. Progress is not saved between sessions.

Main website: https://elden-game-dev.github.io/briarhold/
