A little world with a light to restore

Explore a colourful 3D valley from a fixed, angled viewpoint. Cross bridges, search forest ruins, discover a hidden garden and enter a castle guarded by the Hollow Warden. Collect coins, open chests and use healing hearts and speed potions along the way.

Defeat the guardians near each Dawn Sigil to recover it. With all three sigils, the castle gate opens. Defeat the Warden, approach the beacon and press E to restore its light.

How to play

WASD or arrow keys
Move relative to the screen.
Space
Swing your sword.
Shift
Dodge. Watch for the Warden’s warning ring.
E
Open nearby chests or relight the final beacon.
P or Escape
Pause or resume. Sound can be switched off using the on-screen button.

Before you begin

This single-player game is designed for a desktop or laptop with a keyboard and a browser supporting WebGL 2. No account or installation is required. Touch and gamepad controls are not supported in this version. Progress lasts for the current session; reloading starts a new journey.

Original graphics and sounds are created in code. The game contains no advertising, tracking scripts or in-game purchases.
